Decisions & Status

Summary of key project decisions and current status.

Core Policies

  • Clone-Complete: Primary data and retained artifacts remain in Git.
  • DVC-Light: DVC is used for metadata/dependencies, not for hiding raw data.
  • No Git LFS: LFS is not used to ensure simple cloning.

Pending "Needs Decision" Items

Data movement is blocked until these items are fully audited and resolved:

  • csv/: Excel-derived legacy CSV extracts; migration blocked pending validation of historical extraction script/logic.
  • museum-yama-web/mountains.json: Provisional legacy cache. The legacy/manual filename mountains-merged.json is not canonical; the reusable part is the schema/record shape. The future filename and final output path remain undecided.
  • gpx/annotated/: Legacy unvalidated artifacts. Preserve as historical evidence.
  • gpx/merged-by-year/: Legacy reporting artifacts. Must be validated.

Two-Track Roadmap

Future work is split into two distinct tracks:

  • Website Improvements: Low-risk, incremental enhancements to the presentation layer.
  • Directory-Structure Improvements: Higher-risk changes to the underlying repository structure. This track is currently blocked. No physical data movement may occur until comprehensive source coverage and source-to-target mapping audits are complete.

For more details, refer to the canonical source: docs/migration/website_and_directory_roadmap.md.
